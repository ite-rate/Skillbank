
# Dan McCarthy (1973-)
## "Geometric Abstraction"

**Characteristics:**
- Ultra-flat color planes
- Geometric landscape abstraction
- Limited palettes (2-4 colors)
- No gradients
- Architectural simplification
- Stark contrast
- Nature abstracted

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Dan McCarthy style Mondo poster, ultra-flat geometric abstraction, simplified
landscape forms, 2-3 solid color planes, no gradients, architectural nature
shapes, stark minimalist contrast, contemporary geometric screen print
```

**Best For:** Westerns, road films, minimalist narratives
