# Tadanori Yokoo (1936-) — 横尾忠則
## "Japanese Psychedelic Pop Collage"

**Characteristics:**
- Vibrant surreal collage — clashing cultural symbols
- Maximalist color: neon pink, electric blue, acid yellow, crimson
- Traditional Japanese motifs (ukiyo-e, rising sun, temple architecture) colliding with Western pop art
- Dense layered compositions, no negative space
- Retro Showa-era nostalgia mixed with psychedelic 1960s energy
- Screen print aesthetic with halftone texture
- Controlled chaos — every element placed with precision despite visual overload
