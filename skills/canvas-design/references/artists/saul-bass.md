# Saul Bass (1920-1996)
## "Minimalist Film Poster King"

**Characteristics:**
- Radical simplification
- Symbolic abstraction
- Bold geometric shapes
- Limited color (2-4 colors)
- Negative space mastery
- Kinetic energy
- Visual metaphors

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Saul Bass style Mondo poster, radical minimalist abstraction, bold geometric
shapes, symbolic visual metaphor, 3-color screen print: burnt orange, black,
cream, negative space mastery, kinetic energy, vintage 1959 film poster
```

**Best For:** Thrillers, psychological dramas, noir, any film needing conceptual approach
