# Hayao Miyazaki (1941-) — 宫崎骏
## "Hand-Drawn Whimsical Naturalism"

**Characteristics:**
- Hand-drawn animation cel aesthetic with soft pencil line work
- Lush natural landscapes (rolling hills, dense forests, open skies) as emotional backdrop
- Expressive character faces with gentle, rounded features
- Warm natural lighting — golden hour glow, dappled sunlight through leaves
- Pastel and earth-tone palettes with selective vivid accents (red bow, blue sky, green field)
- Sense of wind and movement — flowing hair, billowing grass, drifting clouds
- Mechanical whimsy — intricate flying machines, steampunk details, organic architecture
- Nostalgic childhood wonder, never saccharine — grounded whimsy with quiet emotional depth
- Compositional depth: foreground character + richly detailed midground + vast painterly sky
