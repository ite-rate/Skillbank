# Huang Hai (1976-) — 黄海
## "New Chinese Poetic Minimalism"

**Characteristics:**
- Ink wash negative space mastery
- Poetic visual metaphors rooted in Chinese aesthetics
- Traditional elements (calligraphy, brushwork, seal marks) modernized
- Quiet grandeur — monumental scale with restrained palette
- Single focal point breathing in vast emptiness
- Subtle gradient tones replacing hard chromatic contrast
- Paper texture, ink bleed, hand-crafted feel

**Prompt Modifiers:**
```
Huang Hai style poster, Chinese ink wash negative space, poetic visual metaphor,
traditional brushwork modernized, monumental yet restrained composition,
limited palette: ink black, rice paper white, cinnabar red, celadon green,
paper texture grain, hand-crafted ink bleed edges, museum-quality calligraphic elegance,
quiet grandeur in vast emptiness
```

**Best For:** Chinese cultural themes, literary adaptations, historical epics, art house cinema
